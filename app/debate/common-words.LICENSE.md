# Common English words

`common-words.json` is adapted from [FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), using its [2018 English frequency data](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/en/en_50k.txt), derived from OpenSubtitles2018.

The data is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), as stated in the upstream README. This adapted data file remains under that license.

Changes: kept the first 3,000 unique lowercase alphabetic words or contractions in frequency order; removed counts and serialized as JSON. Retrieved 2026-10-18. These are surface forms from subtitle frequency data, including names and inflected forms; membership is a learning aid, not a CEFR assessment or an Oxford 3000 classification.
