# NATURAL ENGLISH — 12-MONTH VISUAL-FIRST CURRICULUM

**52 weeks × 6 days × 60 minutes = 312 hours.** Target: CEFR B1 and ≥80% correct listening-comprehension questions on unseen familiar conversations, not an automatic guarantee. American English, image/audio-first lessons, with 90% Oxford-3000-oriented authored vocabulary and exceptions for genuine situations.

Two independent mixes: **48 minutes listening-speaking + 12 minutes reading-writing**, and **48 minutes Everyday English + 12 minutes Dale Carnegie-derived interaction** each study day.

## Daily 60-minute pipeline

| Clock | Minutes | Task | Skills | Content |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 00:00–08:00 | 8 | Review with listening and active oral recall | L/S | Everyday |
| 08:00–23:00 | 15 | One large visual scene; natural audio; gist/details; reveal transcript | L | Everyday |
| 23:00–30:00 | 7 | Sound discrimination, pronunciation and shadowing | L/S | Everyday |
| 30:00–38:00 | 8 | Interactive spoken questions, role-play and feedback | L/S | Everyday |
| 38:00–48:00 | 10 | Read a mini-dialogue; write 1–3 sentences | R/W | Everyday |
| 48:00–54:00 | 6 | Hear a Carnegie-inspired illustrated encounter | L | Carnegie |
| 54:00–58:00 | 4 | Respond aloud to the other person | L/S | Carnegie |
| 58:00–60:00 | 2 | Read and write a one-line reflection | R/W | Carnegie |

## Six-day repeating weekly cycle

1. Monday — Discover: 3–5 reusable chunks and first illustrated dialogue.
2. Tuesday — Understand: audio with alternative wording and new voices.
3. Wednesday — Speak: substitutions and spontaneous answers.
4. Thursday — Apply: use old phrases in a different everyday task.
5. Friday — Listen naturally: speakers and speeds vary, no subtitles first.
6. Saturday — Challenge: unseen audio, uncued dialogue, error diagnosis.
7. Sunday — Rest: no compulsory work.

## Adaptive review pipeline

**Capture → First listen → Understand → Active recall → Spaced review → Error tagging → Transfer to a new scenario → Mastery.**

Illustrative repeat intervals: +1, +3, +7, +14, +30, +60 days, adapted to performance. Failure on audio recognition should trigger a short contrasted replay and an early retry; failure to respond should trigger a new speaking situation. Review queue never increases total time above 60 minutes; reduce new items as needed. Mastery requires audio comprehension and oral use in previously untrained prompts, not memorization of a transcript.

## Visual app design

1. Visual home path: image-led scenes and a 52-week journey.
2. Scene screen: one large relevant illustration taking ~65–75% of the *learning card* viewport, simple play / replay / microphone controls.
3. Audio-first: image + sound before English subtitle; allow optional captions, IPA and Vietnamese after an attempt.
4. Conversation: large illustrated characters, voice reply, feedback and follow-up questions.
5. Review card: front=visual and sound, back=English sentence plus optional IPA and Vietnamese.
6. Carnegie mini-scene: social situation illustrated; respond aloud, then brief reading/writing reflection.
7. Progress: comprehension on unseen clips, pronunciation issues and due review items.
8. Accessibility: captions toggle, alternative text, high contrast, keyboard support. Avoid unrelated decorative art.

## Assessment

- Baseline Week 1, small weekly assessment Day 6, major checkpoint Weeks 13/26/39/52.
- Listening KPI: ≥16/20 answers correct across multiple new familiar everyday clips at a defined difficulty, no captions; repeat with an independent equivalent set.
- Assess B1 listening/speaking/reading/writing separately using CEFR-aligned descriptors; internal 80% score alone is not B1 certification.
- Watch retention and spontaneous response, not just flashcard streaks.
